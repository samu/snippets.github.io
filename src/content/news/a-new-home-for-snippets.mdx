We’ve been making a little space of our own: a new home for Snippets on the web.
It brings the app, the guides, and the stories behind it a little closer
together.

## A clearer way to explore.

The homepage introduces the building blocks of Snippets, from a simple note to
collections, dashboards, and tasks. You can see how they fit together before you
build a workspace of your own.

Light and dark appearances give the site the same familiar feeling, whichever
you prefer.

## A guide when you need one.

The [documentation](/docs/) has its own home, with guides to help you get
started and find your way around. Begin with the basics, or go straight to
something you want to try.

## And a place to keep in touch.

You’re reading it. News is where we’ll share longer stories, product
announcements, and release notes. Each has its own place, so you can get a quick
overview or spend a little more time with the details.

Take a look around. And if something is missing, we’d love to
[hear from you](mailto:support@snippets.ch).
