**Snippets is now available for iPhone on the App Store.** You can take your
notes and tasks with you, whether you’re capturing something on the go or
picking up work you started at your desk.

This launch comes with a complete redesign, a new logo, and a rebuilt website.
Here’s what has changed, and where we’re heading next.

## A new logo, still a note

The original blue note is still recognizable in the new logo. We wanted to keep
that connection while giving it a more distinct shape.

The new logo is a sheet of paper with a lifted corner and a blue underside. It’s
closer to something you could pick up, unfold, and write on. On the
[homepage](/), that idea becomes an animation: the note opens as you scroll,
then settles into the header.

<LogoComparison />

## A complete redesign

We’ve reworked the interface across the editor, navigation, collections, and
dashboards. The aim is to make your content easier to read and the controls
around it easier to recognize.

In the editor, the changes are in the details: darker text, quieter checkboxes,
and softer backgrounds for links to other notes. Color still helps distinguish
content, but fewer elements compete for attention at once.

Those decisions carry through to larger workspaces. Task lists use lighter
separators, attributes have filled labels, and completed tasks are easier to
distinguish from open ones. In the sidebar, color gives familiar folders and
collections a more recognizable identity.

The redesign keeps the same building blocks: notes, attributes, collections, and
dashboards. A task can still live inside a note. A collection can still show the
same content as a list, a table, or a timeline. The new appearance brings those
pieces closer together.

## A new website and documentation

The website now shows more of the app itself. You can explore the editor, task
management, journaling, and flashcards through actual workspaces, then follow
links to the relevant guides.

We’ve also reorganized the [documentation](/docs/) so you can go straight to a
feature or start with the basics. And this News section gives announcements,
release notes, and longer articles a permanent home.

## What’s next

The iPhone launch is an important step, and there’s more work ahead. Our focus
is on the things that make Snippets useful day to day:

- **A better mobile experience.** Keep improving capture, navigation, and
  editing on smaller screens, informed by how people use the iPhone app.
- **More choice for sync.** Snippets Cloud and self-hosted sync are on the
  roadmap, alongside the existing local-file and GitHub options. They are still
  in development.
- **Better connections between features.** Make it easier to move between
  writing, planning, and reviewing without having to rebuild the same setup in
  several places.

We’ll share the details here as those changes become ready. The priorities
remain the same: your notes stay yours, and you choose how to organize them.

If you’re new to Snippets, [take a look at what it can do](/#features). If
you’ve been using it for a while, we’d love to
[hear what you think](mailto:support@snippets.ch) of the new design and the
iPhone app.
