Version 0.22.0 connects date attributes with calendars and reminders, and adds
more ways to describe when something should happen.

## New features and improvements

- **Calendar and reminder sync, in beta.** Sync date attributes with Google
  Calendar and macOS Reminders.
- **Duration expressions.** Specify a time range or duration in natural
  language, such as “Tuesday 9–11” or “Tuesday at nine for two hours.”
- Refined natural-language date parsing.
- Updated the layouts of account pages.
- Reserved space for unset icons on either side of list cards, preventing
  content from jumping when an icon changes.
- Corrected labels in the mobile app’s general settings.

## Bug fixes

- Natural-language date expressions now resolve to a future date consistently.

## Internal notes

- Started preparing the calendar view.
