This example shows how a Snippets release can be presented: the highlights
first, followed by a short, scannable list of changes. These are illustrative
entries, not the actual changelog for version 0.22.0.

## New

- **A quicker place to start.** A small introduction to Quick-Add makes it
  easier to turn a passing thought into a task.
- **A little more guidance.** Helpful links bring the relevant documentation
  closer to your workspace.

## Improved

- Clearer labels in collection settings make it easier to find the view you
  want.
- More consistent spacing gives notes and tasks a little extra breathing room.
- Empty states offer a useful next step when a collection has no notes yet.

## Fixed

- Tidied up the alignment of long titles in narrow sidebars.
- Made keyboard focus easier to see when moving between controls.

## Get started

Visit the [download section](/#download) for the available Snippets apps, or
read the [Quick-Add guide](/docs/quick-add/) to get more familiar with capturing
tasks.
